# ASCII Sharks Wallpaper 0.1.2

November 3, 2026 · Build 3 · Apple Silicon · macOS 13 or later

This version gives the quiet ocean a little more life while restoring space between its small fish.

## What changed

- **The ocean responds.** Move the pointer to part nearby fish. Ripples appear only when you touch small fish. Linger near a passing shark for about 0.6 seconds and it may approach, then return to its patrol. Turn Gentle Mouse Interaction off from the menu whenever you prefer.
- **Recognizable personalities.** In Mixed, hammerheads favor horizontal patrols, makos occasionally accelerate for about three seconds, and threshers turn and move their tails more slowly.
- **Occasional discoveries.** An existing small school may cross the open water, or one uncommon shark may pass in pale ink behind the residents. At 1× speed, opportunities start after roughly a minute and leave long quiet gaps. Active pointer play can postpone them.
- **A calmer composition.** Small fish travel in loose groups of at most seven, with wider spacing and softer ink. The total scales from 24 to 64 with viewport area; encounters do not add more small fish.
- **Desktop behavior stays native.** Clicks pass through the wallpaper, keyboard focus stays with other apps, and pause, hiding, Reduce Motion, and power-saving policies continue to apply.

## Install or update

1. Quit the running version from the fish icon in the menu bar.
2. Extract `ASCII-Sharks-Wallpaper-0.1.2-arm64.zip` and move the app to Applications, or use `ASCII-Sharks-Wallpaper-0.1.2-arm64.pkg`.
3. Open ASCII Sharks Wallpaper. Existing scene, language, display, speed, and playback preferences are preserved. Gentle Mouse Interaction defaults to on if you have not saved that preference before.

The app uses ad hoc signing. Developer ID signing and Apple notarization are not configured; other Macs may prompt for confirmation of the app's source. The installer does not automatically launch the app. This release does not add Intel Mac support or automatic updates.

## Release contents

The release folder contains ZIP and PKG artifacts, individual checksum files, `SHA256SUMS.txt`, these notes in both languages, and `release.json` with the exact source commit, build number, toolchain, and verification results.

From the release folder, verify the files with:

```sh
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt
```

Source checks cover twenty simulation cases and the native WebKit/menu harness. Multiple-display hot-plugging and real sleep/lock transitions still require target-environment testing.
